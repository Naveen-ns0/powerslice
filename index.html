How to use PowerSlice

  1. Enter your total power and pick its unit.
  2. Say whether some power is already on these coins.
  3. For each coin you mine, enter its price in USD, block reward, league power and block time in seconds.
  4. Press Calculate to see how much power each coin should get.

Frequently asked questions

How does PowerSlice find the best power split?

It treats each coin as a separate reward pool. Your share of a coin is your power divided by the league power, so every extra bit of power on one coin earns a little less than the one before. PowerSlice spreads your power until no move between coins can earn more.

Do I need to fill in every coin?

No. Leave a coin empty and it is skipped. Only coins with price, block reward, league power and block time filled in are included.

What if I already have power on some coins?

Choose Yes under the allotted power question and enter how much power you have on each coin. PowerSlice subtracts it from the league power to find what other players hold, then plans your whole power again from scratch.

Does PowerSlice use live prices or live league data?

No. You type in the numbers from your game screen and a coin price source, so check them before you rely on the result. Rewards and league rules can change.

Do I need to log in, and is PowerSlice official?

No login is needed and the numbers are used only to calculate your result. PowerSlice is an independent fan-made tool and is not affiliated with RollerCoin.
