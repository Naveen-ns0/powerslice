About PowerSlice

What you can do here

  • Enter your total power in units from Gh/s up to Zh/s.
  • Add the coins you mine: BTC, ETH, DOGE, SOL, POL, LTC, BNB, TRX, XRP, ALGO, USDC and RLT.
  • Get live coin prices that you can edit, or type your own.
  • Say whether power is already on these coins, and PowerSlice plans your whole power again.
  • See how much power each coin should get and the estimated USD per day.

The idea

RollerCoin players often split their power by feel or by copying others, even though a coin's pool gets less rewarding the more power you add to it. PowerSlice turns that into a quick calculation so the split is based on numbers.

The vision

Simple, free and honest tools for the RollerCoin community: no login, no paid plans and no hype about earnings. PowerSlice is built to be clear about what it assumes, so players can check the result against their own game screen.

How to use PowerSlice

  1. Enter your total power and pick its unit.
  2. Say whether some power is already on these coins.
  3. Add each coin you mine from the list, check its price, then enter its block reward, league power and block time in seconds.
  4. Press Calculate to see how much power each coin should get.

More free RollerCoin tools: PowerShare

PowerSlice decides where your power should go. PowerShare helps you decide what power to add in the first place. It is a free RollerCoin calculator from the same builder, with three tools:

A simple way to use both: check a purchase in PowerShare before you spend, then bring your new total power to PowerSlice to find the best split across coins.

Frequently asked questions

How does PowerSlice find the best power split?

It treats each coin as a separate reward pool. Your share of a coin is your power divided by the league power, so every extra bit of power on one coin earns a little less than the one before. PowerSlice spreads your power until no move between coins can earn more.

What is the best way to split power between coins in RollerCoin?

The best split depends on each coin's price, block reward, block time and how much power other players have on it. PowerSlice puts those numbers together and shows how much power each coin should get for the highest USD per day.

Why not put all my power on the highest paying coin?

Because your share of a coin falls as you add power to it. After a point, the same power earns more on another coin, so the best result is usually a spread across several coins.

Why does PowerSlice sometimes leave a coin at 0%?

When a coin is crowded or its reward is small compared with the others, any power you give it would earn more somewhere else. In that case the best split leaves it out.

Do I need to add every coin?

No. Add only the coins you mine. Each coin you add needs a price, block reward, league power and block time.

What if I already have power on some coins?

Choose Yes under the allotted power question and enter how much power you have on each coin. PowerSlice subtracts it from the league power to find what other players hold, then plans your whole power again from scratch.

Where do I find league power, block reward and block time?

Use the numbers your RollerCoin screen shows for each coin and league. League power is the total power in your league for that coin, and block time is how many seconds one block takes.

Does PowerSlice use live prices or live league data?

Coin prices are fetched from a free public price source and you can edit them. League power, block reward and block time come from you, so check them against your game screen. Rewards and league rules can change.

Why are USDC and RLT prices fixed at 1?

PowerSlice values one USDC and one RLT at 1 USD, so their price fields are locked. Every other coin price can be edited.

Which power units does PowerSlice support?

You can enter total power and league power in Gh/s, Th/s, Ph/s, Eh/s or Zh/s. Results are shown in the unit that fits best.

How often should I recalculate my split?

Recalculate when coin prices or league power change a lot, when your total power grows, or when you move to a different league.

Are the results guaranteed earnings?

No. They are estimates based on the numbers you enter and a simple model of how rewards are shared. Real earnings can differ.

Do I need to log in, and is my data saved?

No login is needed. The numbers you enter are used only to calculate your result and are not tied to an account.

Is PowerSlice official?

No. PowerSlice is an independent fan-made tool and is not affiliated with RollerCoin.
